## Scope and evidence (2026-09-09)

Finding 07 uses the review's scope-narrowing option for physical LAG support.
Encoding, configured numeric checks, and learned-ID consistency are implemented;
physical interface/member association, steering, and ingress verification are not.
The Gap and Partial rows above explicitly remain unsupported. No live LAG setup
or test is claimed. Sender learning is per sender run; reflector session identity
includes the sender micro-session ID (finding 02).

YANG mapping augmentation is outside RFC 9534's scope. That does not make the
MUST to transmit on the associated member link inapplicable. Earlier evidence
that treated external hashing as satisfying that requirement was insufficient.
